Privacy Policy — My 360 Storage
Last updated: 2026
Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy describes how the My 360 Storage app handles your information and permissions on your Android device.
Collection and Use of Personal Data
My 360 Storage does not collect, store, track, or share any personally identifiable information. You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal information to use the app.
Internet Connection Usage
The app is designed to operate primarily offline. Network connectivity is used exclusively to:
Download game cover art and visual assets from public web repositories.
No user or device information is transmitted during the cover art search—only the game identifier/name is queried to locate the corresponding image.
Requested Permissions
To manage files via OTG, the app requests the following permissions:
Storage Access / All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_WRITE_STORAGE): Used strictly to read, transfer, rename, organize, and delete game files, DLCs, save data, and updates on internal storage and external USB drives (flash drives and hard drives).
Network Access (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used solely to query and download game cover art.
Local Storage and Security
All your files, games, and modifications remain stored locally on your device or your flash drive/external hard drive. The app does not upload any personal or game files to external servers.
Third-Party Sharing
We do not share, sell, or transfer any data with third parties, advertising networks, or data analytics platforms.
