# 🇺🇸 English

## 🎮 My 360 Storage — Manage Games, DLCs, Saves & Updates via USB OTG

**My 360 Storage** is an Android application that lets you **manage, install, and organize Xbox 360 games, DLCs, saves, and updates** directly on a **USB flash drive or external HDD** connected to your phone via **USB OTG cable** — no PC or Xbox 360 console needed!

Say goodbye to complicated folder structures. This app **automatically places files in the correct Xbox 360 directory structure**, supporting **GOD**, **XBLA**, and **XEX** formats so your console recognizes everything instantly.

---

### ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 🎯 **Auto-Install Games** | Installs GOD, XBLA, and XEX games directly into the correct Xbox 360 folders |
| 📦 **DLC Installer** | Automatically places DLC files in the proper content directories |
| 💾 **Save Management** | Install and manage game saves with correct folder placement |
| 🔄 **Update Installer** | Apply game updates (title updates) to the right location |
| 🖼️ **Game Library View** | Browse installed games with **cover art** and **game names** |
| 📋 **DLC & Save Viewer** | See all installed DLCs and saves per game |
| 🗑️ **Uninstall Games** | Remove games, DLCs, or saves directly from your USB drive |
| 📁 **File Manager** | Basic file manager — delete, copy, paste, and **backup to phone storage** |
| 📱 **No Root Required** | Works on any Android device with USB OTG support |

### ⚠️ IMPORTANT WARNINGS — Read Before Using!

#### 🚨 DO NOT FORMAT your USB drive when Android asks!

> When you connect your Xbox 360-formatted USB drive to your Android phone, **Android will NOT recognize the Xbox 360 file system**. You'll typically see this happen in three steps:

<p align="center">
  <img src="/format-warning-1-select-drive.png" alt="Step 1: Select the pendrive/HDD via OTG" width="200"/>
  <img src="/format-warning-2-notification.png" alt="Step 2: Android shows a drive issue notification" width="200"/>
  <img src="/format-warning-3-dialog.png" alt="Step 3: Android asks to format the USB drive" width="200"/>
</p>

> 1. You select your pendrive/HDD in the app
> 2. Android shows a notification like *"Issue with [your drive] USB drive — Tap to fix"*
> 3. Tapping it opens a dialog offering to **format the USB drive**
>
> **⛔ DO NOT TAP "Format USB drive"!** If you format the drive, **ALL your Xbox 360 games, saves, DLCs, and data will be permanently lost!**
>
> ✅ **Simply dismiss/cancel this dialog and ignore the notification.** The app handles the Xbox 360 file system internally and does not require Android to natively mount the drive.

#### 🔌 External HDD Power Requirements

<p align="center">
  <img src="/usb-hub-power.jpg" alt="Example of a powered USB hub setup" width="500"/>
</p>

> **External HDDs consume more power** than USB flash drives. Your phone may **not be able to supply enough power** to an external HDD through USB OTG alone.
>
> If your HDD is not detected or keeps disconnecting, use a **powered accessory** such as:
> - ✅ A **USB 3.0 hub with an external power adapter**
> - ✅ A **USB-C hub with Power Delivery (PD)** input
> - ✅ A **SATA-to-USB adapter** with a dual-USB power cable
> - ✅ Or an **external HDD with its own power adapter**
> - ✅ USB flash drives typically work without extra power

---

### 📋 Requirements

- Android device with **USB OTG support**
- **USB OTG cable/adapter** (USB-C or Micro-USB depending on your phone)
- USB flash drive or external HDD **formatted by an Xbox 360 console**
- Xbox 360 game files in **GOD**, **XBLA**, or **XEX** format

---

### 🚀 How to Use

1. **Connect** your Xbox 360 USB drive to your phone via USB OTG
2. **Ignore** any Android format warning — just dismiss it
3. **Open** My 360 Storage
4. **Browse & Install** — Select games, DLCs, saves, or updates to install
5. **Manage** — View your installed game library with cover art
6. **Backup** — Copy files to your phone's internal storage as backup
